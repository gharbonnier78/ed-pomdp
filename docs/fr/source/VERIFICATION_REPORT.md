## Defects found in the previous v1.9 PDF

The earlier page-level assembly was not acceptable for reviewer hand-off because it contained two unintended changes:

1. the sentence explaining scalarisation was partly hidden on v1.9 page 11;
2. the Annex E title on v1.9 page 24 contained broken punctuation (`?`) and was positioned as an overlay.

The corrected reproducible build removes both defects by inheriting the corresponding v1.8 pages unchanged.

## Intentional editorial changes only

1. New first-reading contract and bounded glossary.
2. Cover version update and notice that Annex G is published separately.
3. Extraction of former Annex G into an advanced companion with two new introductory pages.

## Conclusion

The corrected split is genuinely conservative. Apart from the new front matter, the cover notice and the structural export of Annex G, the guide content is preserved. The advanced content itself is not rewritten: it is reproduced page-for-page in the companion.
