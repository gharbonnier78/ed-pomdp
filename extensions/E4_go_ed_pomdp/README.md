## Relationship to E4 v0.1

The v0.1 synthetic study is not rerun, tuned or reinterpreted by this update. Its dispositions remain unchanged:

- explicit goal conditioning changed the first evidence action only in a narrow budget-one case;
- GO-ED-POMDP and the goal-agnostic policy were identical in 10 of 12 held-out cells;
- the two differing five-year-value cells had adverse GO-ED point estimates and intervals crossing zero;
- deliberately wrong-goal conditioning produced a narrow harm signal for customer trust under budget 1;
- broad goal-conditioned decision-loss superiority remains unsupported.

The new outer framing loop, reframing triggers and adaptive-governance demonstration are theoretical extensions marked `NOT_TESTED`. They must not be treated as validation of industrial effectiveness or production qualification.

## Naming boundary

`E4` denotes this conceptual extension. It must not be confused with repository roadmap Step 4, which concerns governed shadow deployment and evaluation.
