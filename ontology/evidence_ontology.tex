\section{Purpose}
The ontology defines the minimum auditable vocabulary connecting engineering claims, evidence, risks, and decisions. It is conceptual and serialisable; it is not yet an OWL standard.
\section{Core entities}
\begin{longtable}{p{0.18\textwidth}p{0.66\textwidth}}
\toprule Entity & Definition\\\midrule
Claim & A proposition about system behaviour, quality, compliance, or readiness.\\
Risk & Uncertain adverse consequence linked to assets, scenarios, likelihood, and impact.\\
EvidenceAction & A controlled activity intended to produce evidence.\\
EvidenceItem & Immutable artefact resulting from observation, analysis, review, or execution.\\
Observation & Value interpreted by an observation model; not identical to the evidence artefact.\\
EvidenceQuality & Latent or assessed properties: validity, reliability, freshness, completeness, representativity, reproducibility.\\
Environment & Configuration and operating conditions under which evidence is produced.\\
DependenceLink & Shared cause or reuse relation that can make evidence non-independent.\\
Constraint & Non-compensatory condition limiting feasible decisions or actions.\\
BeliefSnapshot & Versioned probability distribution and model uncertainty at a decision time.\\
Decision & GO, CONDITIONAL GO, NO-GO, abstain, escalate, remediate, or acquire evidence.\\
Policy & Versioned mapping from belief and context to an action recommendation.\\
AuditRecord & Hash-linked record of inputs, versions, reasoning outputs, and authority.\\\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
\section{Relations}
A Claim \emph{isSupportedBy} EvidenceItem. EvidenceItem \emph{isProducedBy} EvidenceAction and \emph{wasProducedIn} Environment. EvidenceItem \emph{observes} Observation and \emph{hasQuality} EvidenceQuality. DependenceLink \emph{connects} EvidenceItems. Risk \emph{threatens} Claim. Constraint \emph{restricts} Decision. BeliefSnapshot \emph{uses} EvidenceItems and ModelVersion. Policy \emph{recommends} Decision. AuditRecord \emph{records} the complete chain.
\section{Evidence lifecycle}
Planned $\rightarrow$ acquired $\rightarrow$ validated $\rightarrow$ admitted $\rightarrow$ consumed $\rightarrow$ stale/superseded. Rejected evidence remains in the ledger with rejection reason and is never silently deleted.
\section{Machine-readable profile}
The repository contains a JSON Schema defining identifiers, provenance, timestamps, hashes, dependency links, admissibility, and quality assessments. Future versions may map to PROV-O, OpenTelemetry semantic conventions, assurance-case vocabularies, and test-management schemas.
