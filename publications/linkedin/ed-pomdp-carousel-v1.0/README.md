## Publication decisions intentionally preserved

- The visible slide numbering is preserved exactly as prepared for LinkedIn. It is thematic rather than a strict sequential pagination.
- The dates printed on individual pages reflect the production chronology and were not harmonised for this publication snapshot.
- The last two technique maps are intentionally dense and are best read by opening the PDF or full-resolution images.
- The final PDF was normalised without stretching to a single A4-portrait media box. Three source pages with a 2:3 canvas were centred on A4 with white margins.
- Page 8 of the PDF, visibly numbered `5 / 10`, contains the reviewed correction: **“Evidence Can Improve Decisions Over Time.”**

These observations are retained so they are not lost, but they do not block the LinkedIn publication:

3. In Conclusion 2, prefer **“can support better decisions, lower risk, and higher value”** over a categorical promise to deliver better decisions.
4. A future academic or conference edition should use one continuous page sequence and harmonised dates.
