\section*{Scientific reading of Step 2}

The principal mechanism finding is that probabilistic improvement was usually decision-inert. Of 1,119 ED-POMDP-versus-baseline pairs with improved Brier score, 1,054 retained the same terminal action. The risk-only comparison produced the same terminal action in all 480 pairs despite different evidence traces and posterior values in many cases.

The conclusion is therefore stronger than a generic null result: the frozen terminal architecture often failed to convert belief improvement into changed action and reduced loss.

\section*{Milestone 2R commitments}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Audit threshold derivation, loss asymmetry, stopping, cost, dependence, redundancy, and boundary reachability.
  \item Replace broad claims with conditional hypotheses H3-A to H3-E.
  \item Keep Step 2 headline seeds out of all tuning.
  \item Use new development seeds for exploration and untouched seeds for confirmation.
  \item Keep Brier and ECE secondary to terminal loss, unsafe GO, and total evidence cost.
  \item Block industrial progression until both theory and data gates pass.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Approval condition}

Approval of Milestone 2R means approval of the bounded reset and its progression gates. It does not mean approval of ED-POMDP operational superiority, industrial readiness, or the candidate hypotheses.
